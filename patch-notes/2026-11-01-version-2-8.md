# 🤝 Version 2.8: Real players and friendship streaks

- 🎬 **Legend and champion intros:** every legendary, mythical and League Champion gets its own entrance.

Also: the Day Care opens at 10 species and its Eggs take after their parents · offline earnings from level 0 · your Versus best on your Trainer card · a reroll lesson for new trainers · patch notes in French on Discord · fixes.
