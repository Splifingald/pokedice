# 🎡 Version 2.4: the Fortune Wheel's Porygon jackpot

- 🎟️ **Fortune Wheel, reworked:** spins now take tickets. You get a free one every 12 hours and can hold up to 4; while you hold fewer than 4, you can buy more for ₽200 each. The new slices pay ₽50 to ₽1,000, Great Balls, Ultra Balls and a Rare Candy.
- 🎰 **The jackpot moves on:** its slice gives Porygon at 10 %. Once you've caught it, the slice becomes a shiny Porygon at 1 %, then a Master Ball at 0.25 % for good.
- 🃏 **The Game Corner closes for now:** no more slot machine cards. Porygon comes from the Fortune Wheel instead.
- 🎒 **A new Bag button:** a pixel backpack replaces the potion in battles and raids.
- 🔧 **Fix:** Mega Gems bought at the Mart no longer vanish when you restart the game.
