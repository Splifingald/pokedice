# 💀 Version 2.6: Extreme mode, the speedrun

Race every region against the clock.

- 💀 **Extreme mode:** opens after Kanto's League, from the trainer menu. Race each region you've beaten from its starter to its League, on a clock that never stops, and restart to beat your best time on the board. A pure race: no events, shinies or General upgrades.
- 🏥 **Its Pokémon Centers give a boon instead of a heal:** pick 1 of 3, from a team heal to Team Rocket's surprise trade.
- ⚡ **Z-Moves:** a Pokémon with two types picks which one its Z-Move uses.
- 🏆 **Versus league:** clearer opponent and group lists, and a new gold look for the Raid Ball.
