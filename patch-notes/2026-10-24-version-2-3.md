# 🎲 Version 2.3: one status face is enough

- 🎯 **Status faces:** a single face now triggers any status. Poison is 5 % for 5 turns; Freeze and Paralysis are a chance per face (sure at 3 and 2 faces).
- ⚔️ **Raids, reworked:** every legendary raid has its own level, rules and weather, plus its own entrance. Once you have Korrina's badge, 8 legendaries come back as Mega and Primal raids, now the only way to get their gem or orb.
- ✨ **Shiny hunting:** the Shiny Charm, a new General upgrade, raises wild shiny odds up to 1 in 333, and beating a region's League opens its shiny Pokédex page.
- 🏆 **Elite Rebattle on the leaderboard:** the tiers you've cleared now count on the Progression board, and the Hall of Fame needs all four.
- 🎃 **Halloween 26 Pokémaniac:** a new trainer look for anyone who owns a shiny Giratina. Haunted Hollow ends on 1 November.
- 🏠 **Tidier screens:** a cleaner Home with a GO TO button, bigger dice on tall phones, an Inbox in the trainer menu, and pop-ups one at a time.
- 🔧 **Fix:** your save is sent as soon as you hide or close the game, so progress from the phone app is no longer lost.
