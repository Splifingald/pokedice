# Version 2.3: one status face is enough

Status faces got simpler: a single face now triggers any status, so there are no more pairs to chase. Home is also tidier, your dice are bigger on tall phones, and this update fixes a cloud sync problem that could lose progress.

## Status faces, reworked

- **One face is enough** for every status. A status face still counts as its number for damage and combos.
- **Poison:** 5 % of max HP for 5 turns (it was 10 % for 3 turns and needed 2 faces).
- **Freeze:** each Freeze face gives a 1-in-3 chance, so 3 faces freeze for sure.
- **Paralysis:** each Paralysis face gives a 50 % chance, so 2 faces paralyze for sure.
- **Confusion:** more faces, more confusion: 10 %, 20 % or 30 % recoil for 1, 2 or 3+ faces. A lighter confusion never lowers a stronger one.
- **Heal:** heals the Heal faces' value plus that die's upgrade (it used to need 2 faces).
- Burn works as before. The battle log says when a Freeze or Paralysis chance misses.

## New

- **Halloween 26 Pokémaniac:** a new trainer look, the Poké Maniac dressed as shiny Giratina. Catch Haunted Hollow's shiny Giratina in its Distortion World raid to win it, and it's yours for good. If you already own a shiny Giratina, you get it too. Haunted Hollow ends on 1 November.
- **Inbox:** the trainer menu has an Inbox row for your messages, with a count of the answers and gifts waiting for you.

## Changes

- **Battle:** your dice are bigger on tall phones, and REROLL and ATTACK sit lower, right above the Bag. The damage shows once the last die lands and reads simply "26 DMG". Dice you picked to throw again show a ↻ tag and combo dice get gold corners, so colour isn't the only clue.
- **Home:** it only shows squares for things you can use, and a "Coming up" line says what opens next and how close you are. When you're behind in the story, the big red button reads **GO TO** the next area (**Stay here** keeps your free play), and after clearing an area the victory card offers **GO TO NEW AREA**. The Areas list has one red GO: the area you're headed to.
- **Pop-ups** come one at a time, in order. The Mega, Gigantamax and Z-Move rules, which sometimes never showed, now do. The leaderboard tip can be closed.
- **Upgrades:** the buy buttons are navy, and the cheapest upgrade you can afford is gold with a "Best buy" tag.
- **Settings:** one Disconnect, and one Delete button that asks what to delete.
- **Type hints** are on until your first badge, unless you've already chosen.
- **New players:** the name is filled in for you, Prof. Oak's text sits at the bottom of the screen, and the theme follows your device.
- **Trade links** now come with a message naming the Pokémon, its level and what you want for it.
- **Tablets** can play sideways.
- **Toasts** stay up longer when they're long, and error messages stay until you close them.
- **Offline?** Friends, Trades, the leaderboard and Versus now say so instead of "Try again later".
- **Screen readers:** each page has its own title, and moving to a page puts the focus on its heading.
- **Reduced motion:** the Game Corner reels no longer flicker.
- **Encounter preview:** with a team of one there is nothing to pick, and long names show in full.

## Fixes

- **Cloud sync:** hiding or closing the game now sends your save straight away. Progress made in the home-screen app on your phone is no longer lost when you open the game somewhere else.
- A Poké Ball now wobbles fewer times the further a throw falls short, so the third wobble no longer gives a miss away.
- The super-effective sound now plays during attack animations too.
- The gold on the victory card now counts up.
- Evolutions, gifts and hatchings no longer play two jingles at once.
- Buttons react as soon as your finger touches them. The Bag, team, history and Upgrades buttons now click too.
- The intro now describes the controls as they are, and nothing mentions the old Map any more.
