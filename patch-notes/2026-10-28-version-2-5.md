# 🏆 Version 2.5: the Versus league

A weekly league to climb, and a smoother game all round.

- 🏆 **The Versus league:** a new league every Monday. Pick one of 3 opponents near your trophies and fight the match yourself (or on AUTO), with no items. A win earns +3 to +10 trophies, a loss costs as much, and below 100 trophies a loss costs nothing. Climb your group of 20: at the end of the week you get ₽1 a trophy, and the top 10 win Rare Candies and the new **Raid Ball** (+5 to the catch die, in raids only).
- ✨ **UI and UX improvements across the game:** clearer, faster screens on phone and PC, with fewer taps and pop-ups.
- 🥚 **Eggs** now use the Black and White sprite.

## Fixes

- 🌙 Dark mode keeps text readable on coloured buttons and pictures.
- 🖼️ Pokémon sprites still show when Showdown's server is slow or down.
- 🎡 The Fortune Wheel's rays no longer cover its buttons.
