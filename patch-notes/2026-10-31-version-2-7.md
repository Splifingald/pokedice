# 💎 Version 2.7: Hidden talents

Every Pokémon family now has a hidden talent.

- 💎 **Hidden talents:** 40+ talents, one per Pokémon family, unlocked when you find one that carries it in the wild. A gem marks every Pokémon that has its talent, and in battle each one shows the moment it acts.
- ⚡ **Speed by level:** Speed is now the Pokémon's real base Speed plus its level, so an overlevelled Pokémon moves first.
- 🦴 **Shiny fossils:** a fossil now has 1 chance in 10 to be shiny.
